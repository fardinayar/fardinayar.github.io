🏆 **Best Paper Award!** Our paper "LiDAR-Camera Fusion for Video Panoptic Segmentation without Video Training" received the Bronze Best Paper Award at the International Conference on Intelligent Computing and its Emerging Applications (ICEA2024) in Tokyo, Japan.

---

This achievement represents a significant milestone in our research on autonomous vehicle perception systems. The work demonstrates novel approaches to combining LiDAR and camera data for robust video panoptic segmentation without requiring video-specific training.

## Key Contributions

- **Multi-modal Fusion**: Developed an effective framework for combining LiDAR point clouds with camera images
- **Video Segmentation**: Achieved video panoptic segmentation without video-specific training
- **Autonomous Vehicles**: Demonstrated practical applications in autonomous driving scenarios
- **Conference Recognition**: Received Bronze Best Paper Award at ICEA2024

## Impact

This recognition highlights the importance of our research in advancing autonomous vehicle technologies and multi-modal sensor fusion approaches. The methodology has potential applications in various autonomous systems requiring real-time scene understanding.

---

The award ceremony took place during the conference in Tokyo, Japan, where we presented our work to an international audience of researchers and practitioners in the field of intelligent computing and autonomous systems.

> "This award recognizes the innovative approach to LiDAR-camera fusion and its practical applications in autonomous vehicle perception systems."
> —Conference Committee

We are grateful for the recognition and look forward to continuing our research in computer vision and autonomous systems.

I am a Computer Vision and AI Researcher specializing in Autonomous Vehicles, LiDAR-Camera Fusion, and Deep Learning. Currently serving as a Research Associate at the Autonomous and Intelligence Systems Lab (AISLab) at Amirkabir University of Technology, Tehran, Iran.

## Research Focus

My research interests span across several cutting-edge areas in computer vision and machine learning:

- **LiDAR-Camera Fusion**: Developing novel approaches for combining LiDAR and camera data for robust perception in autonomous systems
- **Panoptic Segmentation**: Working on advanced segmentation techniques for autonomous vehicles
- **Deep Learning**: Exploring neural networks for 3D object detection, depth estimation, and scene understanding
- **Graph Neural Networks**: Investigating GNNs for various computer vision applications
- **Autonomous Systems**: Contributing to the development of robust autonomous vehicle technologies

## Academic Background

I hold an M.Sc. in Computer Engineering with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics from Amirkabir University of Technology (2020-2023), where I graduated with a GPA of 18.75/20, ranking 1st among 22 students. My thesis on "Panoptic Segmentation using LiDAR and Camera for Autonomous Vehicles" received the Best Thesis Award.

Previously, I completed my B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from Shahid Chamran University of Ahvaz (2015-2019) with a GPA of 18.04/20, ranking 3rd among more than 40 students.

## Current Work

As a Research Associate at AISLab, I mentor students and lead research projects including:
- Cross-view Generalization for Large-Scale Neural Radiance Fields
- Open-set Scene Graph Generation with Vision-Language Models
- Auxiliary Modality Learning for Autonomous Vehicles
- LLM Agents for Autonomous Driving
- End-to-end LiDAR Localization
- Data-Driven Robot Localization Estimation

I also collaborate remotely with the Spatial Dynamics Lab at University College Dublin on metric monocular depth estimation models for monitoring systems.
